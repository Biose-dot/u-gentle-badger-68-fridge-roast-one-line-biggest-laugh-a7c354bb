Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb) competition **"Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh"**.

`main.py` (pure Python 3 stdlib) copies the one-line roast in `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`.

> At 3 a.m. my smart fridge updated its firmware, then refused to make ice until I accepted the terms — my cubes are now held hostage by a checkbox.

The line is 146 characters (UTF-8, within the 1–200 limit). It roasts the fridge itself — its firmware update and its ice maker — with a fresh angle: not the fridge ordering groceries or watching you, but a terms-and-conditions checkbox holding the ice cubes hostage at 3 a.m.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
